Apex Striker Career - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Overview
Apex Striker Career ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you play Apex Striker Career.

2. Information Collection & Usage
Apex Striker Career is a strictly offline, single-player football simulation game. We do NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII), device identifiers, location data, contacts, telemetry, or behavioral analytics.

3. Local Data Storage
All career progress, match records, player statistics, national team history, settings, and save slots are saved locally and exclusively on your Android device via Room (SQLite) database files (`apex_career_slot_<slotId>.db`) and local SharedPreferences. No game data ever leaves your device or is transmitted to any external servers or third-party cloud infrastructure.

4. Third-Party Services & SDKs
Apex Striker Career contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero tracking pixels, zero analytics frameworks, and zero remote data beacons.

5. Children's Privacy
Our game is suitable for general audiences of all ages and does not knowingly collect, store, or process personal data from children or any other users.

6. Changes to Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected within updated versions of the application and documentation.

7. Contact Us
If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at Apexstrikersim@gmail.com.
