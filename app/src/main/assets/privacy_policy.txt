Apex Striker Career - Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Overview
Apex Striker Career ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you play Apex Striker Career.

2. Information Collection & Usage
Apex Striker Career is an offline, single-player game. We do NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal identifiable information (PII), telemetry, location data, device identifiers, contacts, or user analytics.

3. Local Data Storage
All game states, career records, save slots, and settings are saved locally on your device using Android's SQLite/Room database and SharedPreferences. No data ever leaves your device or is transmitted to external servers.

4. Third-Party Services & SDKs
Apex Striker Career does not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or data analytics services.

5. Children's Privacy
Our game is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any personal data from children or any other users.

6. Changes to Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in updated versions of the game.

7. Contact Us
If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please consult the app settings or documentation.
