2. Game Description
Apex Striker Career is a single-player, text-driven offline football player career simulation game. Players create a striker, navigate through a multi-phase career arc (Street Football, Youth Academy, Senior Professional Club, and International National Team representation), participate in a 12-month calendar season (including domestic league, cup, European tournaments, and quadrennial World Cup competitions), train skills against independent attribute ceilings, negotiate dynamic transfers, and establish multi-generational family legacies.
